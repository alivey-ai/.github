<summary><b>How does a plushie come alive?</b> &nbsp;<sub>(click to expand)</sub></summary>
<br>

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A[Photo of your plushie] --> B{Safety check}
    B -- ok --> C[3D model]
    B -- not ok --> X[Rejected, nothing stored]
    C --> D[Pick a voice]
    D --> E[Talk to it]
    E --> F[It remembers you]
```

1. **Snap** one photo of the toy you love.
2. A safety check runs before anything else happens.
3. We build a 3D version of *your* plushie, not a generic one.
4. Give it a voice and talk to it. It remembers what matters to you.

</details>
